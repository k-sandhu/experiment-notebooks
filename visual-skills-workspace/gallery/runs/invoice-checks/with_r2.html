When does a cheap first pass pay for invoice payments?

A cheap AI model answers every invoice; a check decides which answers to trust, and only the doubtful ones go to an expensive model. Six short steps on 72 test invoices, then a price slider to find where each strategy wins.

Data: 72 generated test invoices from experiment 21 (econ/21-verification-costs: summary.csv, case_results.csv, break_even.json, cost_scenarios.csv). Cheap model: Qwen3.8 Flash (reasoning off). Expensive model: GPT-6.1 Sol. Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5 at list price.

Assumed, not measured: the price of one GPT call (the subscription reported no charge). The checks met no hard mistakes on this set, so the accuracy of the two checks could not be compared. Bills for 1,000 invoices scale the 72-invoice averages and assume the same mix.
