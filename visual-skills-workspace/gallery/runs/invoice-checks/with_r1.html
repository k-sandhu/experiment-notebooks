When does a cheap first pass pay off?

A cheap AI model answers each invoice, a check decides whether to trust it, and doubtful invoices go to an expensive model. Step through seven short steps built on the 72 test invoices from econ notebook 21, then set the expensive model's price yourself.

Data: 72 synthetic test invoices with exact answers (econ/21-verification-costs). Cheap model: Qwen3.8 Flash. Checker: Claude Opus 5.5 at list price. Expensive model: GPT-6.1 Sol, whose price per call is assumed, not measured. Costs per 1,000 invoices are the 72-invoice averages times 1,000.
