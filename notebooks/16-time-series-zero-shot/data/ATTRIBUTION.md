# Data attribution

IESO demand: Copyright © 2004-2022 Independent Electricity System Operator, all rights reserved. This information is subject to the Terms of Use set out in the IESO's website (www.ieso.ca).

The source is the IESO Hourly Zonal Demand Report. The normalized table selects Ontario Demand and indexes each hour at its start; it does not change demand values. The IESO does not endorse this experiment. Terms: https://ieso.ca/Terms-of-Use

Weather: Open-Meteo Historical Weather API and its underlying weather-analysis providers, CC BY 4.0. Toronto grid-cell temperature and wind values are selected without modification. This is reanalysis/analysis data, not an individual weather station. Source and attribution: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api

Page views: Wikimedia Foundation Analytics API, CC0 1.0. English Wikipedia, all-access, user-agent traffic for Toronto and Artificial_intelligence. Source: https://doc.wikimedia.org/generated-data-platform/aqs/analytics-api/documentation/access-policy.html

Exchange rates: Bank of Canada, Valet API, FXUSDCAD. Daily published values are selected without interpolation; weekends and holidays are omitted. This experiment is independent and is not endorsed by the Bank of Canada. The data are available free of charge from the Bank. Terms: https://www.bankofcanada.ca/terms/
